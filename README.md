# HR-Attrition-Diagnostic

**Objective:** Identify drivers of employee attrition using formula-driven analysis.

**Approach:** Built segmentation logic using IF, COUNTIFS, SUMIFS, and XLOOKUP to analyze attrition by department, salary band, tenure, and manager.

**Key Findings:**
- Sales department has highest attrition (35%)
- Low salary band employees are 3x more likely to leave
- Employees with 1-2 years tenure show highest risk
- Manager Sarah Johnson has 45% team attrition rate

**Risk Segmentation Created:**
- Critical Risk: <1 year tenure + Low salary
- High Risk: ≤2 years + Low salary  
- Medium Risk: Low salary only
- Low Risk: All other employees

**Skills Demonstrated:**
IF, IFS, COUNTIFS, SUMIFS, AVERAGEIFS, INDEX-MATCH, XLOOKUP, Conditional Formatting
